# RenderDragon August 2026 Update: Check Your Music, Then Go Animate

We have a big one for you this month. Two things you've been asking for since forever — a proper way to check whether your music is safe to use, and a background generator that finally feels finished. Let's dig in.

## Looney copyright check is here

You can now check a song's copyright status right inside RenderDragon. No more bouncing between tabs or wondering if you picked the wrong tool.

Upload an audio file (up to 50 MB) or drop in a Spotify link, and the check runs in the background — you'll watch it progress live on screen, so you're never left staring at a spinner wondering what's happening. When it's done, you get a clear result and a history of every check you've run, so you can look back at what you've cleared before.

Find it in a few places:

- The **Music Copyright** tool in the Tools dropdown
- Straight from any **music resource card** in the Resources Hub, via the copyright check button
- Every check gets its own shareable result page, so you can link a buddy straight to what you found

One heads-up: you get **5 checks per day**. It's enough for a normal working session, and it keeps the service fast for everyone.

### Why we built this

If you make Minecraft content, copyright is the thing that gets videos struck and channels demonetized. We wanted you to be able to check before you publish, not after. That's the whole point — publish with confidence.

## The background generator got a glow-up

Our most-loved tool just became a whole lot more useful.

### Search your textures

Picked up a giant texture pack and can't find the one you want? The search is now fuzzy — it finds matches even when you don't spell it perfectly, and ranks the closest ones first. Type "dirt" and you'll get what you meant, not a shrug.

### More resolution options

Added **4K DCI, 5K, and 8K** presets on top of the existing options. Whatever you're exporting for, there's a size for it now.

### Multi-image patterns

You can now select several textures at once and render them as a pattern instead of a single image. Choose from grid, staggered, diagonal, scattered, or random — and the random options are seeded, so you can recreate the same look again instead of rolling the dice each time.

### Animated exports

The headline feature: you can now export a **looping animation** instead of a static image. Pick the Image or GIF/Video output mode and the generator records a smooth WebM loop you can drop straight into your video editor.

Tweak it to taste with new controls for:

- **Texture rotation** — spin the pattern while it loops
- **Animation duration** — how long the loop runs
- **FPS** — for that buttery-smooth or deliberately chunky feel
- **Scroll distance** — how far the pattern travels

## Around the site

- Tool cards on the homepage now have hover panels that show off a preview image — actually see what a tool does before you click it
- The nametag generator lets you pick your font
- Fonts are consistent across the whole site now — same family everywhere, no more jarring switches between pages
- Music resource cards got a cleaner look, with the copyright check tucked into a hover-expanding icon

## The small stuff

- Background generator uploads are capped at 20 files, 10 MB each
- Better keyboard support in the upload drop zone (Tab and Enter work as you'd expect)
- Fixed some fiddly bits: preview sizing now matches your output ratio, the sidebar scrolls on its own, and the Looney UI keeps its pixel corners no matter where you look
- Older browsers are covered too — the generator no longer depends on `randomUUID`
